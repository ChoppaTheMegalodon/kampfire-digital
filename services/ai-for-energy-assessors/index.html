How It Works

We don't replace your assessment software. We automate the rest of your day.

Your FirstRate5 / AccuRate / BERS Pro stays where it is — the accredited modelling is yours. We build the layer around it: the lookups, handoffs, gap-checks, distribution, and summaries that currently eat your assessors' time.

The Knowledge-Base Bot

A wiki of your R-values, U-values, solar absorptance tables, NCC clauses, BASIX certificate requirements, product specs, and house-rules — queryable from Telegram, WhatsApp or your internal chat. Your assessor types "solar absorptance for Monument" and gets 0.73, Dark D classification in under five seconds. Pakistan team queries it. New hires onboard against it. Every gap surfaced becomes a new wiki page.

Quote-Acceptance Auto-Forward

Client accepts a quote → assessor gets the brief, the booking, the client folder, and the checklist within seconds. No more three-day forwarding delays. No more SLA fires.

Plan-to-Checklist Gap Finder

Plans land. Claude reads them, checks them against your intake checklist, and flags missing information before the assessor opens FirstRate5. The chase happens at intake, not a month into the job.

Contractor Distribution

Replace WeTransfer's 3-day expiry pain. Job folders sync to your offshore or in-house team with persistent access and a clear handoff. Stop re-uploading files because the link expired over a weekend.

Energy Summary Auto-Generation

Certificate lands. The bot extracts the data points, runs them through your standard format, and produces the client-facing summary. The assessor reviews and approves — they don't retype.

Wiki Capture On The Fly

Senior assessor answers a question over chat. A /save command turns the answer into a permanent wiki page. Tacit knowledge becomes shared knowledge. Compounds week after week.

FAQ

Questions assessors actually ask.

Do you replace our NatHERS software?

No. FirstRate5, AccuRate, BERS Pro, HERO — they stay. The accredited modelling stays with the accredited tool. We automate everything that sits around it.

How long does setup take?

MVP wiki-bot ships in 1–2 weeks. Phase 1 automations roll out over 2–4 weeks after that. We build incrementally — you see value in week two, not month six.

What does it cost?

Setup from $3,000 (founding-client rate, limited). Standard $6,000. Monthly retainer $1,750. Additional automations scoped individually — typical bundle is $4,500–$8,500 per Phase 2 build.

Will this pass accreditation requirements?

Yes — because the accredited assessment isn't what we touch. The assessor stays accountable for the final certified output. We automate the admin layer.

Why not just buy an off-the-shelf tool?

If a SaaS tool fits, buy it. We build for the gaps the tools miss — workflows between your tools, lookups specific to your jurisdiction, handoffs your team does manually today.

What if our team isn't tech-savvy?

Good. Most of our clients aren't, and that's the point. The bot lives in WhatsApp or Telegram — the same app your team already uses. No new login. No new software to learn. You ask it questions in plain English.

Worth a 30-minute conversation?

No pitch deck. We'll ask what's eating your assessors' time. You'll get an honest read on whether automation makes sense before you spend a cent.
