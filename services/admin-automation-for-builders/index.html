What We Build

The workflows that pay for themselves.

These aren't theoretical. They're built, shipped, and saving hours in client offices right now. Pick the ones that hurt most. Skip the rest.

Quote Follow-Up Sequences

Quote sent → automated nudge at 48 hours → check-in at 5 days → "still interested?" at 14 days. Personalised, on your letterhead, sent at the times that actually convert. Stops quotes dying in inboxes.

Client Intake Forms

Replace the JotForm you've outgrown with conditional logic that adapts to the job type. Form submitted → folder created → checklist generated → assessor (or you) notified. One click goes from enquiry to ready-to-quote.

Subcontractor Distribution

Plans, specs and updates synced to your subbies' folders. Persistent access — no 3-day-expiry WeTransfer pain. Notifications when changes drop. Audit trail for who saw what, when.

Appointment Confirmation

Booking confirmed → confirmation email → 48-hour reminder → morning-of nudge. One-tap confirm. No-show rates typically drop from 15% to 4% within a month.

Email Triage

Incoming email read by AI → categorised (quote request / variation / supplier / spam) → tagged and sorted → urgent items forwarded with a summary. Stops you opening 200 emails to find the three that matter.

Variation & Quote Generation

Standard-format variations and quotes drafted in your template, ready for your sign-off. Cuts the document-prep step that everyone hates from 30 minutes to two.

FAQ

The questions builders actually ask.

Do you replace Xero, Buildxact, or our project software?

No. We build workflows around the tools you already run. Your estimating, accounting and PM software stay exactly where they are — we automate the admin between them: quote follow-up, intake, distribution, reminders.

How long until something's actually working?

The first workflow — usually quote follow-up or client intake — ships in 1–2 weeks. We start with your biggest single time-sink, prove it, then expand. You see it working before you commit to more.

We've already got an admin person. Why do we need this?

Hiring doesn't fix a workflow problem — it just throws a person at it, and the friction reappears in a different shape. Automation handles the repetitive handoffs so your admin can do the work only a human can.

We're builders, not a tech company. Is this too complicated for us?

You don't touch any of it. The workflows live in tools your team already uses — email, your phone, SharePoint. No new login, no training course. If you can send a text, you can use what we build.
