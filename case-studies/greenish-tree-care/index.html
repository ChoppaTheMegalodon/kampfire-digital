The Opportunity

A 20-Year Competitor Is Closing. Their SEO Doesn't Have To.

The Strategy

Three Phases. One Goal: Capture Everything.

Phase 1

Build the SEO Foundation

Before acquiring anything, Greenish needs landing pages to redirect to. Without them, 301 redirects point to thin content and Google treats it as a soft 404. The authority evaporates.

  • 15 location pages — one per suburb served (Noosa, Maroochydore, Caloundra, Coolum, Buderim, Nambour, Cooroy, Bli Bli, Tewantin, Mooloolaba, Doonan, Eumundi, Pomona, Sunshine Beach, Black Mountain)
  • 8 blog articles targeting high-intent local keywords (protected trees QLD, tree removal costs, council approval, storm damage, overhanging tree law, habitat hollows, arborist reports, stump grinding)
  • Technical SEO — LocalBusiness schema on every page, Google Business Profile optimization, XML sitemap, mobile speed audit
Phase 2

Acquire the Domain & Redirect

Google confirms 301 redirects pass 100% of link authority. That means every backlink, every indexed page, every brand search for "arborclimb" flows directly to Greenish.

  • Domain acquisition — negotiate purchase of arborclimb.com.au (closing business = motivated seller)
  • Due diligence — check domain authority, backlink profile, indexed page count, traffic estimates
  • 301 redirect map — every Arborclimb URL mapped to its Greenish equivalent (location-to-location, blog-to-blog, catch-all for the rest)
  • Cloudflare implementation — free tier, zero hosting cost, handles all redirects at the DNS level
Phase 3

Capture the Displaced Customer Base

Beyond SEO, there are real humans who used Arborclimb and now need a new arborist. Meet them everywhere they're looking.

  • Google Ads on competitor brand terms — "arborclimb", "arborclimb sunshine coast" (cheap clicks, high intent)
  • Directory takeover — claim listings on every platform Arborclimb appeared (Yellow Pages, Hipages, True Local, Localsearch)
  • Review generation push — target 50+ Google reviews in 3 months (reviews = #1 local ranking factor)
  • Community outreach — Facebook group posts in Noosa and Sunshine Coast communities
  • Direct referral — negotiate Arborclimb recommending Greenish to their client list as part of the deal
