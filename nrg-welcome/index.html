Welcome to your NRG Knowledge Base.

Five minutes to get set up. After that, every NCC clause, R-value, BASIX rule, and product spec you've ever paid for sits one question away — in Discord, on your phone or laptop, available to the whole team.

What this is

We've built NRG a private knowledge base that the whole team can query in plain English. It runs inside a Discord workspace — like Slack or WhatsApp, but free and built for teams. You ask a question in #wiki-bot, the bot answers from your own documents. You drop a new PDF in #ingest, the bot reads it and offers to add it to the knowledge base.

The wiki is yours, on your OneDrive, fully portable. The bot is the interface that makes it instantly searchable.

Setup — 5 minutes

1

Install Discord

On your phone: download from the App Store or Play Store. On your computer: download from discord.com/download and install. Free account, signup takes 60 seconds. Use your real name — your NRG colleagues will see it.

2

Join the NRG workspace

Click the button below. It opens Discord and adds you to the NRG Knowledge Base workspace.

Join the NRG Discord →

If the button doesn't work, copy this link: https://discord.gg/jUFydS8Erj

3

Say hi in #general

Once you're in, you'll see a few channels on the left. Drop a quick "hello" in #general so we know you're set up. Tyrone will assign you the NRG Team role — that's what unlocks the bot.

4

Ask your first question in #wiki-bot

Try something you'd normally Google. For example:

  • "What's the solar absorptance for Monument?"
  • "R-value of an R1 XPS board?"
  • "What's the WoH heating load cap for climate zone 5?"
  • "NCC 2022 — when does Spec 44 apply?"

The bot reads from NRG's own documents. It cites the source. If it doesn't know, it'll tell you — never guess. (That's a hard rule. Confidence in wrong numbers destroys trust.)

Read the colour, not just the words. Every answer comes back colour-coded:

  • 🟢 Green — answered straight from NRG's documents, sources cited in the 📎 footer. Safe to use.
  • 🟠 Amber — the wiki doesn't have that specific figure. The bot says so and points to the document to check. Verify before quoting to a client or certifier.

Three slash commands worth knowing: /help (quick guide), /reset (clear your chat history with the bot), /status (is it alive, how big is the wiki).

5

Drop a PDF in #ingest when you've got new info

Got a new regulation update, product datasheet, or internal SOP you want the wiki to know about? Drop the file (PDF, Word doc, or text file) into the #ingest channel. The bot reads it, drafts a summary, and shows it for review. Tyrone confirms with ✅ — and the wiki grows. Usually takes about 30 seconds end to end.

The bot's promise

It will not invent numbers. If the answer isn't in the wiki, it says so and points you to the right source document. "I don't have that — check sources/src-X.md" is always the right answer when the wiki is silent.
